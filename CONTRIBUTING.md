# Contributing

The Software-Defined Computing Ecosystem research project welcomes good-faith technical review, criticism, evidence, and alternative approaches.

## Before Contributing

Please distinguish clearly between established facts, derived conclusions, hypotheses, proposed designs, and experimental results. Cite sources where appropriate and identify assumptions and limitations.

## Research Contributions

Useful contributions include:

- Prior-art discoveries
- Literature and technical references
- Falsifying evidence or counterexamples
- Mathematical analysis
- Simulations and reproducible experiments
- Architectural alternatives
- Security analysis
- Economic and incentive analysis
- Protocol and interoperability considerations
- Editorial corrections and documentation improvements

## Discussion Standard

Disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation, harassment, and bad-faith disruption are not.

## Implementation Boundary

This repository is for research and specification. Contributors should not assume that production implementation belongs here. A separate implementation repository will be established if and when the research reaches that stage.

## Attribution

Contributors should make the provenance of substantive ideas and research material clear. Do not submit material that you do not have the right to contribute.

## Licensing and Intellectual Property

No repository-wide open-source license has been selected at this stage. Contributions should not be interpreted as granting rights beyond those that already apply under law or an explicitly stated license. Licensing, patent, trademark, and contribution terms will be established deliberately as the project matures.
