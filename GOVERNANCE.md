# Governance

This repository records the research and development of the Software-Defined Computing Ecosystem (SDCE) concept.

## Stewardship

The project is currently stewarded by its originator, Eric G. Moteberg. As the project develops, governance may evolve to accommodate external contributors, researchers, reviewers, and maintainers.

## Principles

1. Preserve a clear historical record of the project's development.
2. Separate evidence from hypothesis and proposal.
3. Encourage good-faith technical criticism and competing explanations.
4. Credit contributors and sources accurately.
5. Prefer interoperable, decentralized designs where they satisfy the research objectives.
6. Do not treat an implementation as proof that the underlying concept is correct.
7. Major changes should be documented rather than silently rewritten.

## Scope

This governance document applies to the research repository. It does not by itself define licensing, patent rights, trademarks, or the governance of any future implementation project or broader ecosystem.

## Future Governance

If the project attracts substantial external participation, the governance model should be revisited explicitly rather than emerging accidentally through repository permissions or informal convention.
