# Research Method

## Purpose

This repository is the research and specification workspace for the Software-Defined Computing Ecosystem (SDCE) concept. It is not the implementation repository.

## Research Discipline

The project should clearly distinguish among:

- **Established:** supported by reliable evidence or direct observation.
- **Derived:** conclusions that follow from established information and stated assumptions.
- **Hypothesized:** plausible propositions that require testing or further evidence.
- **Proposed:** architectural or policy choices offered for consideration.
- **Demonstrated:** claims supported by a reproducible experiment, simulation, benchmark, or prototype used as a research instrument.
- **Rejected:** approaches or claims that evidence has shown to be inadequate or inconsistent with project goals.

## Evidence Standards

Where practical, research claims should identify their source, date, assumptions, and limitations. Prior art should be documented fairly, including evidence that challenges the SDCE hypothesis.

## Experimental Principle

Experiments and simulations exist to test claims, not to manufacture confirmation. Negative results are first-class research outcomes.

## Repository Boundary

The research repository may contain mathematical models, simulations, benchmark data, diagrams, pseudocode, and small experimental artifacts when they support research. It should not become the home of the production/reference implementation. Software implementation should be maintained in a separate repository when that stage is reached.

## Change Discipline

Major changes to the conceptual architecture, terminology, assumptions, or research direction should be recorded in the repository history and, when appropriate, an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).
