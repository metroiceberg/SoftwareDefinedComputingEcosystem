# Architecture Decision Records

This directory records significant architectural and conceptual decisions made during the development of the Software-Defined Computing Ecosystem research project.

## When to Create an ADR

Create an ADR when a decision materially changes the architecture, terminology, assumptions, scope, research direction, or interpretation of evidence.

## Suggested Format

Each ADR should include:

- **Status:** proposed, accepted, superseded, or rejected
- **Date:** date of the decision
- **Context:** the problem or question being addressed
- **Decision:** what was decided
- **Rationale:** why the decision was made
- **Alternatives:** significant alternatives considered
- **Consequences:** expected benefits, costs, risks, and research implications

## Principle

An ADR records the reasoning at the time of the decision. Later evidence may overturn it. Superseding an ADR should preserve the original record rather than erase it.
