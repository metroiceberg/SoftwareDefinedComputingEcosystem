# Researcher Onboarding

## Purpose

This document provides a lightweight starting protocol for people who join the SDCE research effort.

The project is intentionally designed to welcome independent technical scrutiny before committing to an implementation or a conclusion.

## Start Here

A new researcher should begin with:

1. `README.md` — project orientation.
2. `ENCHILADA.md` — the complete current conceptual picture.
3. `RESEARCH_METHOD.md` — research and evidence discipline.
4. `docs/experiments/PHASE_0_SINGLE_SYSTEM_FEASIBILITY.md` — the logical lynchpin.
5. `docs/experiments/PHASE_0_RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md` — known questions and attack surface.
6. `docs/experiments/PHASE_0_STARTING_POINT.md` — current technical starting position.
7. `docs/experiments/PHASE_0_PRIOR_ART.md` — existing technologies that may overlap with or challenge the proposition.

## The First Assignment

Do not begin by designing the full SDCE ecosystem.

Begin by trying to answer:

> **What is the strongest reason the Phase 0 proposition might be wrong, redundant, impractical, or already solved?**

If that objection can be resolved, identify the next strongest one.

## Useful Contributions

Contributors are encouraged to provide:

- prior-art discoveries
- technical objections
- counterexamples
- alternative models
- operating-system analysis
- hardware analysis
- mathematical reasoning
- benchmark designs
- reproducible experiments
- security concerns
- performance analysis
- terminology corrections
- questions not already documented

## How to Record a Challenge

When practical, describe:

**Claim being challenged** — What proposition or assumption is under examination?

**Objection** — Why might it be wrong, incomplete, redundant, or impractical?

**Evidence** — What supports the objection?

**Implication** — What would change if the objection is correct?

**Possible test** — What experiment or analysis could distinguish the competing explanations?

This structure is a guide, not a bureaucratic requirement.

## Intellectual Posture

The project does not require agreement with the SDCE hypothesis.

A strong negative result is more valuable than a weak positive result.

Contributors should feel free to recommend abandoning, narrowing, reformulating, or replacing parts of the concept when evidence supports doing so.

The goal is not to protect the original idea.

The goal is to determine what survives serious investigation.

## Adding Questions

The Phase 0 Research Questions document is intentionally open-ended.

If you discover an important question that is missing, add it.

We explicitly acknowledge that the initial authors do not know all of the questions, let alone all of the answers.

## Moving Toward Experiment

Once the major objections and existing technologies have been mapped sufficiently, contributors should work toward the smallest reproducible experiment capable of testing the lynchpin proposition.

Avoid unnecessary implementation complexity.

> **Build only enough to test the question.**

## Attribution and Conduct

Credit substantive contributions accurately. Distinguish original analysis from cited material. Follow the repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` guidance for technical contributions, disagreement, attribution, and intellectual property.
